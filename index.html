    Your trusted circle

    These are the people notified by SOS alerts and missed journey check-ins. Add or remove anyone — changes apply immediately to the demos above.

      Why a small circle works better

      Three to five people who will actually answer at 2am beats twenty who won't. Sahel deliberately keeps this list short — every person on it gets full location access during an alert, so choose people you trust completely.
